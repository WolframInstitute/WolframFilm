# ψ — archival clips

The physicists' recorded voices, real experiment footage and period film for the narrated cut of "ψ — the quantum
century".  This file extends `SOURCES.md`; the media listed there (the Messenger 6 "nobody understands" clip at
≈8:02–8:07, Commons Einstein 1943, Tonomura stills, Solvay photos, portraits) is not repeated.  Researched 2026-10-02.

**How it was checked.**  Every direct URL was HEAD-checked (HTTP 200 with a content-length) unless the row says
otherwise.  Licences come from Commons `extmetadata`, archive.org `/metadata/<id>`, the NARA catalog, or the
institution's terms page.  Times and transcripts were checked by one of:

| mark | method | accuracy |
|---|---|---|
| **VTT** | the host's own subtitle file (Lindau, CERN) | wording exact, times ±0.3 s |
| **audio** | the file itself, cut with ffmpeg and speech-recognised (whisper.cpp), spellings fixed by hand | ±0.5 s; check by ear before locking the edit |
| **ASR** | YouTube or archive.org auto-captions, or a hand-made uploader caption track | words right, times ±1 s; re-check on the file you download |
| **shots** | archive.org thumbnail strips (every 30–60 s) or a stock house's shot list | ±30 s; for picture only |
| **UNVERIFIED** | URL and licence checked, the moment not located | — |

Times are positions **in the file linked**.  ARD/SWR MP3s begin with a spoken broadcaster intro, which the times
include.  YouTube copies have their own offsets, different from the originals.

**Rights, in short.**  Almost every *voice* is copyrighted as a recording, even where the speaker's own words have
become public domain.  Planck's words are PD (d. 1947), and Einstein's since 1 Jan 2026.  Pauli's become PD in 2029,
Schrödinger's in 2032 and Bohr's in 2033.  The free voices are Google's CC BY film, and NASA and NIST (US-government)
material.  Everything else needs a licence or a short-quotation / fair-use argument.
