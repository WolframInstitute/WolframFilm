# ψ — the quantum century

A 2:42 film about a hundred years of quantum theory, from Planck's quanta (1900) and Schrödinger's wave equation
(January 1926) to entanglement, the world it built and the computers it is building.  Its protagonist is the wave
function, drawn with its phase as colour; its rhythm is the clicks of measurements, and its score is physics too:
hydrogen's Balmer lines are the chord, measured qubits play the hi-hats, an entangled pair plays kick and clap.
Every picture is solved, sampled or simulated in the notebook.  No generative image, video or music models.
